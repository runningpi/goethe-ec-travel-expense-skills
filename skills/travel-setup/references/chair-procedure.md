# Business-trip procedure at the Chair of Electronic Commerce

Use this to tell the user their **next steps** after every piece of work. Do not put personal
names or e-mail addresses into anything you produce — refer to roles ("the chair's
secretary", "the chair holder", "the dean's office", "the A1 contact at the travel department
(Reisekostenstelle)"); the user knows who that is.

## Before the trip
1. **Travel application** (domestic or abroad form). Enter the cost centre of the project that
   pays (rules.md §7) and a realistic cost estimate.
2. Hand the application to the chair's secretary or the chair holder. It is signed by the
   chair holder and also by the dean's office. **Only travel after it is approved.**
3. Keep the signed application — it is required for the reimbursement.
4. **Trips abroad only (EU and non-EU, e.g. Switzerland, USA): request an A1 certificate** from
   the A1 contact at the travel department by e-mail, before the trip. Draft the e-mail for the
   user (German, below); the user sends it.

### A1 request e-mail (template)
Subject: `Bitte um A1-Bescheinigung – Dienstreise <Ort>, <Datum>`

```
Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,

ich bitte um Ausstellung einer A1-Bescheinigung für die Dienstreise von <Vorname Nachname>
nach <Land, Ort> zur <Name der Konferenz/des Workshops>.

1) Mitgliedstaat, in den die Entsendung erfolgt: <Land>
2) Adresse im Mitgliedstaat (Straße, Hausnr., Postleitzahl, Ort): <Veranstaltungsort mit Adresse>
3) Voraussichtliche Dauer der Entsendung: <TT.MM.–TT.MM.JJJJ>
4) Die entsandte Person ist im Beschäftigungsstaat tätig als: <profile.position>
5) Beschäftigungsstelle(n) während der Entsendung: <profile.workplace>
6) Rentenversicherungsnummer: <profile.social_security_number>

Mit freundlichen Grüßen
<Vorname Nachname>
```
(Replace the salutation with the contact's name if the user gives it — do not store it.)

## After the trip
1. Claim the reimbursement **right after the trip** — after 6 months nothing is reimbursed.
2. The claim needs: complete form with start/end of travel **and** start/end of business, meals
   received, taxi reason if any; **all invoices** (train/flight, hotel, conference fee, local
   transport, taxi); **proof of payment for every invoice** (credit card, bank or PayPal
   statement; cash → receipt); the signed application; the A1 certificate for trips abroad.
3. If the user presented: add the conference programme with **the user's name and talk title
   marked** (assemble.py `highlight`).
4. Optional: a small cost overview table helps the reviewer for trips with many receipts.
5. The user signs (page 1 and annex), then the chair holder checks and signs "sachlich
   richtig"; the dean's office signs as well.
6. After all signatures: put all documents (originals) in an envelope addressed to
   **Hauspost – Finanzabteilung / Reisekostenabrechnung – PF 52** and drop it in the in-house
   post box.

## Next-steps message (end of every workflow)
Finish with a short numbered "Next steps" list tailored to what was just done, e.g. after a
claim: 1) check the PDF, 2) print + sign page 1 and the annex, 3) chair holder signs "sachlich
richtig", dean's office signs, 4) envelope via in-house mail to Finanzabteilung /
Reisekostenabrechnung, PF 52, 5) deadline date (end of trip + 6 months).
